A two-player contest

Roman Numerals

I may step once, II up to twice, III three, IV four, V five — in any direction. Ranks I and VI stay sealed until every piece from ranks II and V stands on III and IV.

How to play

  1. Gold moves first. Click a piece, then a highlighted square.
  2. Each numeral may travel up to that many squares, including diagonals.
  3. You may not jump. Landing on an enemy captures it.
  4. Back-rank pieces (rows 1 and 6) cannot move until every remaining front-line piece (the ones that started on rows 2 and 5) sits on row 3 or 4.
  5. Win by capturing every opposing piece, reaching the far back rank, or leaving the opponent with no legal move.
